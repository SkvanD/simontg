Mac's Perfect Spot

Complete • 12 chapters

Chapter 1: Mac's Cozy Den

Mac is a fox, so bright and so red, With a wonderful tail and a sleepy head. Under the roots of a great, old tree, Lives a cozy home, as sweet as can be.

It's warm and it's soft, and it's tucked away, The perfect place to end a busy day. But Mac has a question, a curious thought, "Is there a better spot that can't be bought?"

Chapter 2: The Owl in the Tree

Mac climbed up high, where the branches are grand, To see how the Owl rules over the land. With a hoot and a stretch in the moonlight so bright, The Owl tucked her wings for the quiet of night.

High in the leaves, in a nest made of wood, She slept just as deeply as an Owl could. "A branch is a bed!" the Owl said with a yawn, "It's the best way to sleep 'til the break of the dawn."

Chapter 3: The Beaver in the River

Mac wandered down where the river runs wide, To see where the Beaver would go to hide. With a splash and a tap and a rhythmic beat, The Beaver built something quite sturdy and neat.

In a lodge made of sticks, in the water so cool, The Beaver slept soundly, away from the school! "A home in the river!" the Beaver did say, "Is the finest place for a watery play."

Chapter 4: The Bear in the Cave

Mac hiked to the mountains, so rocky and steep, To find where the Bear went to wander and sleep. In a cavern so dark, so quiet and deep, The Bear found a place for a long, heavy sleep.

With a snore and a sigh in the shadows so vast, The Bear's winter nap was a shadow long cast. "A cave is so sturdy!" the Bear gave a cry, "As the cold winter winds go whistling by."

Chapter 5: The Monkey in the Jungle

Mac swung through the jungle, so green and so thick, Where the vines and the branches are tangled and quick. A Monkey was swinging, a wonderful sight, Searching for rest in the tropical light.

In a leafy green hammock, high up in the air, The Monkey slept soundly without any care. "A swing in the vines!" the Monkey did cheer, "Is the softest of beds that you'll find around here!"

Chapter 6: The Rabbit in the Meadow

Mac hopped through the meadow, where wildflowers grow, To see where the Rabbit would go to and fro. Deep under the clover, away from the sun, The Rabbit's soft sleeping had only begun.

In a burrow so cozy, so earthy and low, The Rabbit slept soundly, with nothing to show. "A hole in the ground!" the Rabbit did peep, "Is the safest and best place for a little sleep."

Chapter 7: The Penguin on the Ice

Mac traveled to places where winter stays near, Where the ice is so white and the air is so clear. A Penguin was waddling across the cold snow, Looking for somewhere to settle and go.

In a nest made of stones, on the ice far and wide, The Penguin slept soundly with nothing to hide. "The ice is my home!" the Penguin did say, "As I nap through the frost of a cold, wintry day."

Chapter 8: The Elephant in the Grass

Mac wandered the plains, where the sun is so hot, To find a nice place for a midday nap spot. An Elephant walked with a stomp and a sway, Looking for shade at the end of the day.

In a patch of tall grass, so golden and deep, The Elephant settled for a wonderful sleep. "The grass is my bed!" the Elephant said, As she rested her heavy and magnificent head.

Chapter 9: The Hedgehog in the Leaves

Mac crept through the garden, where shadows are long, To hear the soft songs of the cricket's night song. A Hedgehog was rustling, a tiny round ball, Moving quite slowly, not moving at all.

In a pile of leaves, so crunchy and brown, The Hedgehog curled up, far from the town. "A bed made of leaves!" the Hedgehog did sigh, As the autumn wind whispered a soft lullaby.

Chapter 10: The Sloth in the Tree

Mac looked to the tropics, where life moves so slow, To see where the Sloth would decide it should go. High in the canopy, hanging so still, The Sloth moved with grace and a wonderful will.

On a branch that was thick, in the heat of the day, The Sloth hung so softly, in a sleepy kind of way. "To hang by my toes!" the Sloth said with a grin, "Is the best way to let the long slumber begin."

Chapter 11: The Bat in the Cave

Mac flew to a canyon, where darkness resides, To see where the Bat often wanders and hides. A shadow moved quickly, a blur in the air, As the Bat found a corner with plenty to spare.

Upside down, hanging, with wings tucked in tight, The Bat slept so deeply in the middle of night. "To hang from the ceiling!" the Bat gave a squeak, "Is the perfect way for a flyer to sleep!"

Chapter 12: Mac's Perfect Spot

Mac traveled the world, from the ice to the trees, From the deep, darkling caves to the tropical breeze. She'd seen all the beds, from the high to the low, But she felt a strange feeling that started to grow.

She ran to her woods, through the leaves and the dew, To the home that was waiting, so cozy and new. In her den, under roots, in the warmth and the light, Mac curled in a ball for a wonderful night.

"The owl and the bear, and the monkey so grand, Have wonderful beds in this beautiful land. But my den is the best, and my home is the prize, With a sleep that is perfect in my very own eyes!"
