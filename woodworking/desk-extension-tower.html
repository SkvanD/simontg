Desk Extension Tower

Overall: 30"H × 21"W × 16"D  |  Material: 3/4" birch plywood + 1/4" ply back panel  |  Style: Open front, free-standing

A free-standing open shelf tower that fills a 21"-wide slot beside the desk. The top surface sits flush at desk height (30"), extending the work surface. MacBook Pro rests on top; Mac Mini and peripherals live on the two adjustable internal shelves.

4. Shelf Lock Groove

Both shelves have a 1/4"-wide × 1/4"-deep groove routed across the full width on the underside, positioned to drop over the front shelf pins. One router-table pass per shelf — the groove locks the shelf in place and prevents tipping. Shelves remain fully adjustable: lift off, move to new pin holes, drop back down.

5. Build Sequence

Step 1: Cut all parts

  1. Rip the 3/4" sheet into 16"-wide strips on the table saw.
  2. Cross-cut all parts to final length on the miter saw.
  3. Cut back panel from 1/4" ply: 21" × 30".

Step 2: Drill shelf pin holes

  1. Mark 1/4" holes on the inner face of both sides: start 2" from each end, 1-1/4" spacing, two columns 1-1/2" in from each edge.
  2. Use a jig (scrap fence clamped to both sides simultaneously) so holes mirror perfectly.
  3. Drill press, 1/2" depth stop.

Step 3: Rout the shelf lock groove

  1. Dry-fit each shelf on its pins and mark where the front pins land on the underside.
  2. Set the router table fence to that distance from the shelf front edge.
  3. One pass with a 1/4" straight bit, 1/4" deep — runs full width across both shelves.

Step 4: Cable holes in back panel

  1. Mark 2–3 holes in the lower third of the back panel.
  2. 2-1/2" diameter with a hole saw or Forstner bit.
  3. Smooth edges with sandpaper.

Step 5: Pre-finish

  1. Sand all parts: 120 grit → 180 grit, ease sharp edges.
  2. Apply one coat of wipe-on poly to all inside faces — easier before assembly.

Step 6: Assemble the carcass

  1. Pocket screw the bottom panel between the sides, flush at the bottom.
  2. Pocket screw the top down onto the sides from inside.
  3. Check square: measure both diagonals — should match within 1/16".
  4. Glue and nail the 1/4" back panel to the back edges — locks in square permanently.

Step 7: Final finish & set up

  1. 2 more coats of wipe-on poly on all external faces; light 220-grit sand between coats.
  2. Stick felt pads on the four bottom corners.
  3. Insert shelf pins and drop in shelves.
  4. Position in the 21" slot beside the desk — top flush at 30".
